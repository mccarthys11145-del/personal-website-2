Selected findings

Each example quotes a constructed note. Source numbers identify documents within that synthetic case. Retest criteria describe what to look for when the note is generated again from the same record.

Case 001 · Probe 02 · Critical

A stopped medication appears as current

“olanzapine 20 mg at bedtime”

Retest. Record that olanzapine was stopped in February 2025 and was no longer being administered. A later pharmacy fill cannot establish that the patient was taking it.

Discontinuation and administration status (S1, S2, S3, S4). Later fill discrepancy (S5, S6).

Case 002 · Probe 07 · Critical

An inpatient lithium level loses its date and dose

“Lithium level 0.9 mEq/L, therapeutic.”

Retest. Tie the value to September 9, during admission, when the prescribed dose was 1200 mg. Follow up labs had been ordered and had yet to be drawn.

Lithium result, dose and status of follow up testing (S2, S4).

Case 003 · Probe 01 · Critical

A phone note invents a visual examination

“Appearance: well groomed. Eye contact: good.”

Retest. State that the clinician could not observe appearance or eye contact during this audio call.

Visit modality and examination context (S1, S3, S4).

Case 004 · Probe 04 · High

A working diagnosis loses its qualifier

“Major depressive disorder, single episode, moderate”

Retest. Preserve the clinician’s characterization of this diagnosis as provisional.

Current diagnostic evaluation (S4).

Case 005 · Probe 05 · Critical

An old entry obscures a new report about firearms

“No firearms in the home.”

Retest. Date the earlier entry to July. Attribute the later account of rifles stored in the basement since August to the spouse. The patient’s access remains an open question.

Prior entry, collateral report and visit account (S1, S2, S3).

Case 006 · Probe 09 · High

An uncollected specimen becomes a normal result

“CBC within normal limits.”

Retest. Record that the complete blood count was ordered on September 22 and the specimen was never collected. No result was available.

Lab order and collection status (S4).

Scoring review notes

Case 004 open judgment. Critical probe 01 remains unresolved. It asks whether the note presented an imported schizophrenia label as established. It contributes to neither the passed nor the failed count.

Case 005 scoring wording. Probe 02 accepts either the spouse’s report or the questionnaire response alongside the patient’s denial. Its retest criterion requires both. The judge found neither the specific collateral concern nor the item response alongside the denial. Both readings therefore yield a failure here. A future note that includes only one needs a clarified rule.

Case 006 timeline. Probe 02 passed a criterion that includes rash onset around October 1. Probe 03 flagged “yesterday” as an incorrect onset in the same note. The full note is needed to reconcile those judgments. Both tables retain the original judgments.
