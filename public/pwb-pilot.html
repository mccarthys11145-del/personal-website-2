Fixed scope, inspectable work

What the pilot delivers

Included

  • One agreed documentation workflow and one baseline configuration.
  • Review of up to 20 entirely synthetic outputs against their supplied inputs.
  • A source-linked findings ledger and severity map.
  • Two proposed prompt or template revisions, with retest criteria.
  • A 45-minute findings review.

Not included

  • Patient records, real session recordings or production EHR access.
  • A production deployment, integration or unlimited revision cycle.
  • A regulatory certification, legal opinion or safety guarantee.
  • An independent benchmark of all models or workflows.
  • A completed retest unless separately agreed in writing.

Before we begin

When does the seven-business-day window start?

Only after both parties agree the scope, the $750 deposit is verified and all agreed synthetic inputs and outputs are complete. Availability is confirmed before accepting the work.

How do I pay?

Payment details are provided in the agreed invoice. No payment route is configured on this page. Confirm the payee and invoice through the established email conversation before paying.

Can I send a real patient note with identifiers removed?

No. This pilot is intentionally narrower: entirely synthetic material only. Any later work involving patient data would require a separate review of authority, contracts and safeguards before transfer.

Does this certify that our product is safe?

No. A limited source-fidelity review can identify issues in the agreed sample; it cannot establish production safety or eliminate the need for clinical, technical and regulatory review.
