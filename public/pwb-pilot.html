Synthetic pilot scope

What the pilot delivers

Included

  • One agreed documentation workflow and one baseline configuration.
  • Review of up to 20 entirely synthetic outputs against their supplied inputs.
  • A findings ledger linked to the source evidence and severity map.
  • Two proposed prompt or template revisions, with retest criteria.
  • A 45 minute findings review.

Not included

  • Patient records, real session recordings or production EHR access.
  • A production deployment, integration or unlimited revision cycle.
  • A regulatory certification, legal opinion or safety guarantee.
  • An independent benchmark of all models or workflows.
  • A completed retest unless separately agreed in writing.
